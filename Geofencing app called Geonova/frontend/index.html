The Geonova Initiative

India is a tapestry of extraordinary landscapes—from the high glacial passes of Ladakh to the tropical backwaters of Kerala, the royal deserts of Rajasthan, and the living root bridges of Meghalaya.

Geonova was founded to empower domestic and international tourists to experience every corner of India with complete peace of mind. By synthesizing state-wise tourism promotion with real-time geofencing, verified local authorities, 24/7 direct helplines, and an encrypted document vault, Geonova sets a new standard for responsible, secure travel technology.

Statutory Emergency & Privacy Guarantees

  • Consent-Based Tracking: GPS coordinates are only accessed with explicit user permission and can be halted instantly.
  • No False Claims: Emergency SOS coordinates are logged securely with direct connections to official Indian helplines (112, 100, 102, 1090, 1363).
  • Encrypted Document Vault: Travel documents are never exposed through public URLs or unencrypted storage.
  • Emergency Access is Free: Tourists are never charged to view safety protocols or contact emergency authorities.
